Privacy policy

CatchFish · last updated 15 November 2026

What we store

  • Your game progress: currencies, gear, quests, fish collection and similar game data.
  • A random device key the game creates on first launch, so it can sign you in again. We keep only a hash of it.
  • If you sign in with Google: your Google account id and email address, so your progress follows you to other devices. We don't receive your password, contacts or anything else from your Google account.

How we use it

Only to run the game: to save your progress and sign you in. We don't show ads, don't use analytics or tracking, and don't sell or share your data with anyone.

Where it's kept

On the game's own server, operated by the CatchFish developers. Connections to it are encrypted (HTTPS).

Deleting your data

Email CONTACT_EMAIL from the address of your Google account (or tell us your player id) and we'll delete your progress and sign-in data. You can also remove CatchFish's access at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
